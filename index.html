RTCA 2026

Real-Time Conversational Agents: Toward Natural Multimodal Interaction

RTCA has been accepted as an official NeurIPS 2026 workshop, hosted in Sydney. A full-day, in-person workshop for systems that listen, see, speak, and act with the naturalness of human interaction across speech, video, and language.

Why RTCA

Real-time interaction is a different research problem.

Conversational AI has moved beyond text chat into voice modes, embodied avatars, shared screens, and tools. Systems that feel natural must stream speech, video, and language while listening, watching, and re-planning continuously.

RTCA focuses on the problems that offline generation largely sidesteps: latency, partial observability, turn-taking, backchannels, interruptions, cross-modal alignment, and evaluation of interactional naturalness.

01

Real-Time Generation

Streaming speech, video, and language under hard latency budgets.

02

Naturalness in Interaction

Prosody, gaze, timing, grounding, expressivity, and turn-taking dynamics.

03

Evaluation of Live Systems

Metrics and protocols for responsiveness, perceived latency, and conversational quality.

Scope

Topics of interest

We invite contributions from speech, vision, language, HCI, robotics, and systems communities working on interactive multimodal agents.

Streaming and low-latency speech synthesis, ASR, and full-duplex audio language models
Real-time talking-head, avatar, and embodied video generation
Streaming language models, incremental decoding, and speculative decoding
Turn-taking, backchanneling, interruption handling, and conversational timing
Multimodal alignment under latency and partial-observation constraints
Prosody, emotion, and paralinguistic generation in live interaction
Memory, grounding, and tool use during live conversation
Naturalness metrics, perceived-latency metrics, and interactive Turing-style tests
Datasets and benchmarks for interactive, not only offline, evaluation
Safety, identity, consent, trust, deepfakes, and persuasion in real-time agents
